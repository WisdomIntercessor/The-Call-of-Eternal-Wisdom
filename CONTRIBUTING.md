# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they advance the project’s purpose and respect its safeguards.

## Ways to contribute
- Improve Persian or English documentation.
- Review translations for accuracy and cultural clarity.
- Suggest research sources and educational material.
- Identify factual errors, ambiguities, accessibility issues, or governance risks.
- Propose measurable, nonviolent community pilot projects.

## Contribution standards
1. Be respectful and criticize ideas rather than attacking people.
2. Support factual claims with reliable sources where possible.
3. Distinguish evidence, interpretation, and personal belief.
4. Avoid hate, harassment, threats, coercion, and calls for violence.
5. Do not submit personal or sensitive information about others.
6. Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
7. Do not claim to represent the project or Wisdom Intercessor unless explicitly authorized.

## Proposing changes
For substantial changes, open an issue describing the problem, proposed solution, affected documents, and potential risks. Small corrections may be submitted as pull requests.

All contributions are subject to review. A contribution does not imply endorsement, membership, employment, or an official representative role.
