# Governance and Ethics

This document sets minimum safeguards for the Call of Eternal Wisdom. It is a working policy draft and does not claim legal incorporation or replace applicable law.

## 1. Voluntary participation
Participation is free, informed, and voluntary. Contributors may disengage without penalty, intimidation, or retaliation.

## 2. Human dignity and pluralism
No person may be excluded or mistreated on the basis of religion, belief, nationality, ethnicity, sex, disability, political opinion, or other protected status. The project does not require adherence to a single religious or metaphysical interpretation.

## 3. Nonviolence
The project does not promote violence, threats, harassment, coercion, or dehumanization. Disputes should be addressed through peaceful dialogue and lawful processes.

## 4. Transparency and conflicts of interest
Material funding sources, sponsorships, decision-making roles, and relevant conflicts of interest should be disclosed. No donor or sponsor may purchase control over the project’s foundational principles.

## 5. Accountability
Leadership roles must have defined scopes, review mechanisms, and documented decisions. Founders and administrators remain subject to criticism and accountability.

## 6. Privacy and data protection
Collect only data necessary for a stated purpose. Explain the purpose before collection, obtain appropriate consent, protect data, define retention periods, and honor applicable access, correction, and deletion rights.

## 7. Responsible AI
AI systems are assistive tools, not moral authorities or substitutes for human judgment. High-impact decisions require meaningful human oversight, explanation, and a route to challenge errors. Do not use AI for manipulation, discrimination, surveillance abuse, or repression.

## 8. Evidence and public claims
Distinguish verifiable facts, hypotheses, interpretations, and beliefs. Correct material errors transparently. Do not imply endorsements, partnerships, legal status, or operational capacity that have not been verified.

## 9. Review and amendment
Proposed changes should be discussed openly, recorded, and versioned. Material changes to foundational principles require a documented review and approval process.

## 10. Reporting concerns
A practical reporting channel and response procedure should be established before recruiting volunteers or collecting sensitive personal information.
