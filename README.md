# The Call of Eternal Wisdom | صور بیداری حکمت

**A global initiative for human awakening, ethical transformation, and wisdom-centered civilization.**

**Hosted by:** [Wisdom Intercessor](https://wisdomintercessor.com)  
**Project scope:** Global from inception  
**Languages:** Persian and English  
**Status:** Foundational framework — public draft for review

## Purpose

The Call of Eternal Wisdom is a voluntary, nonviolent, human-centered initiative inviting people to cultivate intellectual, ethical, and spiritual awareness and to contribute to fairer, more responsible, and sustainable societies.

The project uses “the call” as a metaphor for awakening and civic responsibility. It does not claim to enact or predict a religious end-time event, speak on behalf of God, or possess exclusive authority over truth.

## Foundational commitments

- Human dignity, equal moral worth, and fundamental rights
- Freedom of conscience, thought, inquiry, and peaceful expression
- Truth-seeking, reason, evidence, and intellectual humility
- Justice, fairness, accountability, and transparency
- Voluntary participation and freedom to leave
- Nonviolence, dialogue, and respect for pluralism
- Environmental stewardship and intergenerational responsibility
- Responsible technology and privacy protection

## Relationship to Wisdom Intercessor

Wisdom Intercessor is the parent ecosystem and host platform. This repository maintains the initiative’s mission, public documents, governance principles, roadmap, and community contributions. It does not create a separate or competing parent identity.

## Participation

Participation is voluntary. Read the charter, review the principles, raise questions, propose improvements, or contribute research and translations. Disagreement and good-faith criticism are welcome.
