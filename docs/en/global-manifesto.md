# Global Manifesto of the Call of Eternal Wisdom
## A Call for Wisdom, Responsibility, and a Shared Future

**Version 1.0 — Public Draft**

We share one world: rich in knowledge and possibility, yet still challenged by injustice, distrust, conflict, environmental degradation, and decisions too often detached from wisdom and responsibility.

A better future cannot be built through technological progress or the accumulation of power alone. It requires people who think critically, ask questions, respect differences, and accept responsibility for the consequences of their choices.

**The Call of Eternal Wisdom** is a global, voluntary invitation: to awaken conscience, cultivate wisdom, connect knowledge with ethics, and turn shared values into responsible action.

The word “call” is a metaphor for human awareness and awakening. It is not a claim to enact a supernatural event, predict the end of the world, speak on behalf of God, or possess exclusive authority over truth.

We commit to these principles:

- The dignity of every person must be respected.
- Freedom of conscience, thought, and inquiry must be protected.
- Truth-seeking requires evidence, dialogue, and humility.
- Justice cannot endure without accountability and transparency.
- Disagreement never justifies hatred, intimidation, or violence.
- Technology should serve people and protect their rights.
- The Earth and future generations matter in today’s decisions.
- Participation must be informed, free, and voluntary.

We invite individuals, researchers, educators, artists, civil-society practitioners, and independent institutions to contribute through criticism, dialogue, education, research, and practical projects.

No individual or institution owns this invitation or has authority over another person’s conscience. Its credibility must be measured by transparency, responsible conduct, and outcomes that can be evaluated.

This movement begins not with a claim, but with a responsibility: to contribute to a wiser future.
